## About

AI Engineer at **Grupo Trimodos**, an intermodal transport and logistics group, where I design internal applications, automation pipelines and AI tooling used in day-to-day operations.

I hold a **Master's in Artificial Intelligence from EDEM (Marina de Empresas)** and come from a background in administration, finance and logistics — which shapes how I work: I start from the business problem, then choose the model, the data pipeline or the automation that actually moves the needle.

**Focus areas**

- **Applied ML & MLOps** — end-to-end pipelines, model monitoring and explainability on Google Cloud
- **LLM applications** — RAG systems, multi-agent workflows and conversational assistants
- **Business process automation** — n8n workflows, API integrations and ERP (Odoo) tooling
- **Internal tools** — full-stack apps and dashboards deployed on self-hosted infrastructure
